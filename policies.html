Our Policies

Return Policy

We want you to love your purchase. If you are not completely satisfied, you may return eligible items within 7 days of delivery.

  • Product must be unused, in original packaging, with all tags attached.
  • Return shipping costs are borne by the customer unless the product is defective.
  • Refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the return.
  • Cash on Delivery orders are refunded as store credit or bank transfer (subject to verification).
  • To initiate a return, please contact customer care within 7 days of delivery.

Shipping Policy

  • Free shipping on all orders above ₹999.
  • Orders are dispatched within 1-2 business days.
  • Estimated delivery: 3-7 business days (depending on location).
  • You will receive a tracking link via email once shipped.

Cancellation Policy

  • Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placement (no questions asked).
  • After 1 hour, cancellations may be subject to a 10% restocking fee.
  • Contact customer care immediately for cancellation requests.

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. Your personal data is used only for order processing and will never be shared with third parties without your consent.

We use secure payment gateways and do not store your card details.
